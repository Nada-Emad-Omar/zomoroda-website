Why Our System is Critical for Space Commercials

1. Fills The Radar Blindspots

Protects Against Untracked Debris

Ground-based radar cannot track space debris smaller than 10 cm. However, a piece of space junk as tiny as 1 cm travels at 28,000 km/h and can completely shatter a satellite. ZOMORODA brings optical cameras directly onto the satellite to detect nearby objects in real-time.

2. Instant Autonomous Avoidance

No Waiting For Ground Control

Satellites usually have to wait until they pass over a ground station to receive collision warnings and burn commands. If a threat appears suddenly, there's no time to wait. ZOMORODA evaluates collision risk and triggers avoidance maneuvers automatically in milliseconds.

3. Massive Cost Reduction

Saves Millions in Mission Operations

Hiring round-the-clock ground analysts to monitor orbital telemetry for every single satellite in a mega-constellation is extremely expensive. By shifting decision-making directly onto the satellite's computer, operational overhead drops drastically.

4. Designed for Low Power (SWaP)

Lightweight & Easy to Mount on CubeSats

CubeSats have very limited power, size, and weight capacity. ZOMORODA is specifically built using lightweight edge AI hardware (2.6 ms inference speed) so commercial satellite manufacturers can add it as a standard plug-and-play safety payload.
