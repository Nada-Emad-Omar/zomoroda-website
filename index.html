Why Onboard Intelligence is Essential for Commercial Space

1. Perception & Detection

Closes Ground Radar Blindspots

Ground systems suffer from orbital prediction uncertainties and latency[cite: 1]. By placing optical vision directly onboard, our lightweight YOLO26n model achieves 98.9% mAP@50 to detect small debris in real time where the threat exists[cite: 1].

2. Real-Time Processing

Autonomous Ultra-Low Latency Decision-Making

Eliminates dangerous ground station communication delays[cite: 1]. Coupling 19% faster YOLO26n inference with Unscented Kalman Filtering and Clohessy–Wiltshire dynamics allows immediate onboard collision risk evaluation and maneuver triggering[cite: 1].

3. Propellant Optimization

Zero Wasted Fuel & Maneuvers

Unnecessary thruster burns deplete non-renewable propellant and shorten mission lifespan. UKF trajectory filtering smooths state estimation up to 2.7×, executing Δv burn commands only when strict collision probability thresholds are breached[cite: 1].

4. SWaP-C Modular Design

Low-Cost Payload for Commercial CubeSats

Designed for resource-constrained CubeSats, our integrated framework combines embedded edge hardware, propulsion control, and an end-to-end safety architecture to protect high-capital assets and ensure LEO sustainability[cite: 1].
